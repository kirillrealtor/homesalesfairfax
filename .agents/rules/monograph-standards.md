# Encyclopedic Monograph Standards (Vienna, Oakton, McLean & Regional Guides)

1. **NO Promotional CTA Cards**:
   - DO NOT add marketing CTA boxes (such as `.doc-cta-card`, "Request a Free Home Valuation", "Browse Active MLS Listings", or promotional links) inside encyclopedic monographs or community guides.
   - The article should conclude after the FAQs with the standard legal disclaimer block (`.doc-disclaimer-box`).
   - Keep the monographs purely authoritative, objective, and reference-grade so readers and search engines recognize them as top-tier educational publications.

2. **Zero Dollar Figures ($)**:
   - Do NOT include specific property sale prices, median home prices, list prices, rents, or price-per-square-foot metrics.
   - Explain structural market mechanisms, absorption velocity, inventory turnover, and architectural typologies instead.

3. **Zero Volatile Ephemeral Rates**:
   - Do NOT include volatile interest rates (e.g., specific mortgage percentages) or temporary annual tax rate numbers.
   - Explain the underlying legal and statutory mechanisms (e.g., Virginia Code Title 58.1 100% fair market value assessments, town vs. county millage, conforming vs. jumbo underwriting).

4. **No Author Biography Blocks**:
   - Keep the content 100% focused on the locality, its architecture, history, and civic systems.

5. **Superiority Over All Competitors (Wikipedia, Grokopedia, Portals)**:
   - Always provide exhaustive, granular detail covering:
     - Exact municipal governance vs. county administration
     - Exact zoning classifications (e.g., RS-10, RS-12.5, R-E 2-acre, lot coverage caps, setbacks)
     - Complete historical chronology from colonial origins to modern infrastructure
     - Hydrology and watershed protections (Chesapeake Bay Act 100-ft RPA buffers)
     - Complete FCPS school pyramids and full-time Advanced Academic Program (AAP) Level IV centers
     - Multi-modal transportation (Metrorail heavy rail, express lanes, W&OD trail)
     - Non-HOA vs. HOA governance and architectural autonomy
